\end{itemize}

\bigskip
\noindent
We have addressed every point raised by the referee.  In summary, the
principal changes in this revision are:

\begin{enumerate}

  \item \textbf{Uncertainties and finite-sample truth baselines on every
        metric.}  All separation-power and Wasserstein-1 numbers in
        Tables~1 and~2 are now reported with bootstrap uncertainties
        ($n=100$), together with a truth-baseline floor obtained by
        splitting the real data into two independent halves.  This
        follows the standard set by Ref.~[58] (Kansal et al., the JetNet
        paper).  Table~1 has been recomputed end-to-end on a single
        self-consistent evaluation run so that the flowBDT and
        truth-floor columns share the same statistics.

  \item \textbf{Standard JetNet 30$\times$3 particle-level benchmark
        (new Table~2).}  We now report bootstrap-aware unbinned $W_1$
        distances on the jet mass, jet $p_T$, and each per-particle
        feature ($\eta^\mathrm{rel}$, $\phi^\mathrm{rel}$,
        $p_T^\mathrm{rel}$), following Ref.~[58].  Two columns are
        reported: the raw flowBDT output and a post-hoc rescaling that
        matches the empirical jet $p_T$ distribution.  The per-particle
        marginals lie within $\sim\!50\times$ the truth floor; the
        residual jet-level gap is discussed honestly as a limitation of
        the per-feature regression strategy.

  \item \textbf{Discriminator-based test for CaloChallenge (new ROC and
        AUC in Section~IV.B).}  An MLP classifier is trained to
        distinguish real Geant4 showers from generated samples on the
        8-feature high-level representation; the bootstrap AUC is
        $0.9995 \pm 0.0002$.  We discuss the near-unity value frankly as
        a consequence of (i) the per-feature MSE objective not directly
        constraining the joint distribution and (ii) min-max
        boundary-clipping artifacts.

  \item \textbf{Quantitative $\tau_{21}$ / $\tau_{32}$ consistency
        check.}  The directly generated $\tau_{21}$ ($\tau_{32}$) is
        compared to the ratio $\tau_2/\tau_1$ ($\tau_3/\tau_2$)
        reconstructed from independently generated components.  The
        $W_1$ discrepancies are $\sim\!0.014$ ($\sim\!0.029$),
        respectively, at the percent level of the underlying ratio
        distributions and within a small multiple of the finite-sample
        truth floor.

  \item \textbf{Figure~8 regenerated from scratch}, fixing the
        overlapping vertical-axis labels noted by the referee.  The new
        figure uses ratio sub-panels and was produced by an end-to-end
        reproduction pipeline (data $\to$ Flow-Diffu training $\to$
        Flow-OT training $\to$ plot) included in the public repository.

  \item \textbf{Clarification of contributions vs.\ Ref.~[47]},
        explicit data-handling paragraph (continuous data,
        zero-padding/mask, min-max scaling), corrected references
        (Kansal, N-subjettiness, ECF, OmniFold Zenodo, GFlash Zenodo),
        renamed Section~IV (``Tasks''~$\to$~``Results''), and a
        thorough language pass.

  \item \textbf{Public code release} with reproduction scripts for all
        new metrics in this revision (\texttt{evaluation/},
        \texttt{scripts/} directories), including the slurm wrappers
        used to produce the numbers in Tables~1 and~2 and Figure~8.

  \item \textbf{Code-availability statement} added to the Conclusion
        pointing to the public repository.

\end{enumerate}

We believe the revised manuscript addresses all the referee's concerns

thorough and helpful report.
